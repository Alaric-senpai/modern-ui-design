## How to study an image

1. Note the structure first: sections in order, column counts, where the CTA sits.
2. Then the tokens: primary colour, tint, radius feel, shadow weight.
3. Then the details worth copying: floating badges, overlapping search bar, highlighted pricing tier, status pills, centre FAB.
4. Rebuild from tokens and patterns; do not try to pixel-copy brand names or logos from the references. Use the user's own brand and content.
